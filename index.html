How a saving is counted

For each preloved item, the price of the same item new (from the Cress price list for that school's phase, branded or plain), less what the family paid. Free items count at their full new price.

Carbon and water

Recorded against each item at the point of sale, using Cress's garment impact factors for avoiding a new item.

Where this comes from

Paid, uncancelled orders from the Cress platform across Nottingham City Council schools, refreshed every weekday morning.
